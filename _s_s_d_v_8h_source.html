10/*
11 SSDV (Slow Scan Digital Video) packet layout, see https://ukhas.org.uk/doku.php?id=guides:ssdv
12
13 Byte Normal (FEC) No-FEC Content
14 0 0x55 0x55 sync
15 1 0x66 0x67 packet type
16 2-5 callsign, base-40 encoded, MSB first
17 6 image ID
18 7-8 packet ID, MSB first
19 9 image width / 16
20 10 image height / 16
21 11 [7:6] 0, [5:3] quality XOR 4, [2] EOI, [1:0] MCU mode
22 12 offset of first MCU in payload (0xFF = none)
23 13-14 index of first MCU, MSB first (0xFFFF = none)
24 15-... 205 B payload 237 B payload re-encoded JPEG scan data
25 ... CRC-32 CRC-32 over bytes 1 to end of payload
26 224-255 RS(255,223) parity - over bytes 1-223
27*/
